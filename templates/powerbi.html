Examiner ko viva me batane ke liye Power BI me banaye gaye official DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) formulas:

1. Total Crimes Reported Measure
Total Crimes = SUM(crime_data[cases_reported])
Selected filters (Year, State) ke according total incidents compute karta hai.
2. Conviction Clearance Rate %
Conviction Rate % = DIVIDE(SUM(crime_data[convictions]), SUM(crime_data[chargesheeted]), 0) * 100
Court trial me case solve hone aur doshi thehrane ki efficiency calculate karta hai.
3. Chargesheet Ratio %
Chargesheet Rate = DIVIDE(SUM(crime_data[chargesheeted]), SUM(crime_data[cases_reported]), 0) * 100
Police dwara charge-sheet file karne ka investigation performance ratio.
4. Average Crime Rate per 100k
Avg Crime Rate = AVERAGE(crime_data[crime_rate_per_100k])
National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) standardized per-capita rate.
